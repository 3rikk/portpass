Behind the map

Portpass combines a public passport matrix with a small set of document rules. It is an exploration tool, not a live border-admission check.

What’s not covered yet

Most third-country visa exemptions, long-stay visa categories, work permissions, bilateral residence agreements, travel history, entry counts, passport validity thresholds and individual restrictions. Unknown residence results mean “not assessed”, not “ineligible”.

Other visitor visas and residence permits are recorded as documents held, subject to their terms. They do not automatically override a “no admission” record. No document numbers, scans or accounts are needed.
