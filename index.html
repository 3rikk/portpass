What’s not covered yet

Family-member rights, UK Withdrawal Agreement status, EU long-term resident and Blue Card mobility, Turkish association rights, most other bilateral schemes, long-stay visas, travel history, entry counts and individual restrictions. These need details the wallet does not collect. “Check eligibility” means approval or passport confirmation is still needed; “Not assessed” does not mean ineligible. Visa facilitation is not a visa waiver, and a UK/Irish residence permit does not confer CTA citizenship rights.

Other visitor visas and residence permits are recorded as documents held, subject to their terms. They do not automatically override a “no admission” record. No document numbers, scans or accounts are needed.
