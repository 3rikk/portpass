Your wallet is private

The passport, visa and residence-permit details you enter are stored only in this browser on this device. Portpass never receives or stores them on its servers. There is no account or cloud sync. Clear this site’s browser data to erase your saved wallet. If you choose to share a map image, the details shown in that image are visible to its recipients.

What’s not covered yet

Other family-member rights, UK Withdrawal Agreement status, EU long-term resident and Blue Card mobility, unassessed Turkish association exceptions, most other bilateral schemes, long-stay visas, unrecorded travel history, entry counts and individual restrictions. Visa clocks estimate stays from the dates you enter; they do not check border records or confirm admission. Other eligibility rules need additional details. “Check eligibility” means approval or document confirmation is still needed; “Not assessed” does not mean ineligible. Visa facilitation is not a visa waiver, and a UK/Irish residence permit does not confer CTA citizenship rights.

Other visitor visas and residence permits are recorded as documents held, subject to their terms. They do not automatically override a “no admission” record. No document numbers, scans or accounts are needed.
